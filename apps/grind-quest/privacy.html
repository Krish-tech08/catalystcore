Introduction

Grind Quest ("the App") is an independent mobile application developed by CatalystCore Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, stores, and protects information while you use Grind Quest.

Offline-first by design

Most user data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to dedicated Grind Quest servers.

Information We Collect

Grind Quest does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Files
  • Messages

The App allows you to create a custom Hunter Name. Your gameplay and productivity progress associated with the Hunter Name is stored locally on your device.

Local App Data

To provide its gameplay and productivity features, Grind Quest stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Hunter Name
  • XP and levels
  • Rank progress
  • Quest history
  • Daily and weekly challenge progress
  • Coins
  • Unlocked personas
  • Reward inventory
  • Active boosters
  • Notification preferences
  • Reminder schedules
  • App settings

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to a dedicated Grind Quest application server.

How Information Is Used

Information stored by Grind Quest is used to provide and maintain the App's functionality, including:

  • Tracking quests, XP, levels, and rank progression
  • Maintaining challenge and streak progress
  • Managing coins, rewards, personas, and boosters
  • Scheduling reminders and productivity notifications
  • Saving your app preferences and settings

The locally stored gameplay and productivity data is not uploaded to or processed by a dedicated Grind Quest server.

Notifications

Grind Quest may request notification permission in order to provide optional productivity and gameplay notifications, including:

  • Quest reminders
  • Daily challenge reminders
  • Streak reminders
  • Productivity notifications
  • Reward notifications

Notification schedules and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can manage notification permissions through your device's operating system settings.

Third-Party Services

Grind Quest currently uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect certain device, advertising, and usage information necessary to provide, personalize where applicable, and measure advertisements. This information is handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Grind Quest does not operate its own advertising, analytics, or gameplay-data server.

Advertising

Rewarded ads are optional

Watching a rewarded advertisement is entirely optional and may provide in-app rewards such as coins or other gameplay benefits.

Grind Quest may display optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect information from your device in connection with advertising services. The collection and use of such information is governed by Google's own privacy policy and applicable settings and controls.

Data Storage and Deletion

Grind Quest stores its gameplay and productivity data locally on your device. The App does not currently provide cloud synchronization or upload this data to a dedicated Grind Quest server.

Because this information is stored locally, uninstalling the App or clearing its app data may permanently remove locally stored Grind Quest progress, depending on your device and operating system's backup behavior.

Children's Privacy

Grind Quest does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require users to provide personal information or create an account.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected unintentionally from a child, we will take appropriate steps to remove that information where reasonably possible.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Most Grind Quest gameplay and productivity data remains on your device rather than being transmitted to our servers.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Users are responsible for protecting access to their own devices and for maintaining appropriate device security.

Future Changes to the App

Future versions of Grind Quest may introduce optional online features, cloud synchronization, or additional third-party services. If such features materially change how information is collected, used, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those features become available, as applicable.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Grind Quest evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
