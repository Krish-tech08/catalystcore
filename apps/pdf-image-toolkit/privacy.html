PDF & Image Toolkit Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

PDF & Image Toolkit ("the App") is developed and provided by CatalystCore Studios ("we", "us", or "our").

We designed PDF & Image Toolkit with privacy in mind. The App's core PDF and image processing features are designed to process your files locally on your Android device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App and its third-party services may process.

1. Your Files and User Content

PDF & Image Toolkit provides tools for working with PDFs, images, scanned documents, and related files.

Depending on the features you use, the App may process files for purposes such as:

  • PDF creation and manipulation
  • PDF merging
  • PDF compression
  • Image conversion and processing
  • Document scanning
  • OCR/text recognition
  • Barcode or QR recognition
  • Other file-processing utilities provided by the App

Your PDFs, images, scanned documents, and other User Content are processed locally on your device. We do not upload your User Content to our servers, and we do not collect or store the contents of files you process using the App.

The App does not operate a server that receives your documents or images for processing.

Files created or modified by the App are saved to a location selected or authorized by you on your device.

2. Information We Collect

Although the App's file-processing functionality operates locally, we use certain third-party services to support advertising, understand App usage, process purchases, diagnose technical problems, and improve reliability.

Depending on how you use the App and the services enabled in the App, information that may be processed can include:

  • App usage and interaction information
  • App version and installation information
  • Device and operating-system information
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Advertising-related identifiers and information
  • Information relating to advertisements and interactions with advertisements
  • Purchase and Premium entitlement information

We do not intentionally send the contents of your PDFs, images, scanned documents, or other User Content to our advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or payment services.

3. Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its functionality and user experience.

Analytics may process information such as app events, usage patterns, device and app information, and identifiers associated with the App installation or session.

For example, analytics may help us understand which tools are used, how features are being used, and whether changes to the App improve the user experience.

Firebase's data practices are described in Google's documentation:

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Android Data Disclosure

4. Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes and other technical problems.

Crashlytics may process information such as:

  • Crash reports
  • Stack traces
  • Application state relevant to a crash
  • Device information
  • Operating-system information
  • App version information
  • Crashlytics installation identifiers
  • Technical diagnostic information

This information helps us identify bugs and improve the stability of the App.

We do not intentionally attach the contents of your PDFs, images, scanned documents, or other User Content to crash reports.

More information is available in Google's Firebase Crashlytics documentation.

5. Advertising

PDF & Image Toolkit is supported by advertising.

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. These services may process certain information about your device, App usage, advertising interactions, or advertising identifiers in order to serve, measure, personalize, or improve advertisements, subject to applicable laws and your device or platform settings.

Your PDFs, images, scanned documents, and other User Content are not provided to advertising partners by the App for the purpose of serving advertisements.

Advertising providers may have their own privacy policies describing how they process information.

If Google Mobile Ads / AdMob is used in the App, additional information about Google's advertising practices is available through Google Privacy & Terms.

6. Google ML Kit

The App may use Google ML Kit for features such as OCR/text recognition and barcode recognition.

Where the applicable ML Kit functionality supports on-device processing, recognition is performed on your device rather than by our own servers.

ML Kit functionality and data practices may vary by API and implementation.

For additional information, see the Google ML Kit documentation.

7. Camera Permission

The App may request camera access when you use document-scanning or camera-based functionality.

Camera access is used to capture documents or images that you choose to scan.

Captured content is processed for the requested operation and saved according to your selections.

The App does not use the camera for continuous monitoring.

8. File Access and Storage

The App may use Android's Storage Access Framework and related file-selection mechanisms.

When you select a file using Android's file picker, the App receives access to the file or location that you explicitly select or authorize.

The App does not require unrestricted access to all of your personal files merely to process an individual file.

You remain in control of which files you provide to the App.

9. Premium Purchases

PDF & Image Toolkit offers an optional Premium upgrade through a one-time purchase.

The Premium purchase is not a recurring subscription and does not automatically renew.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

The App may receive information necessary to verify your purchase and determine whether Premium functionality should be available to you.

We do not directly collect or store your full payment-card information.

Payment information is handled by Google Play according to Google's applicable policies and terms.

10. Premium Pricing

Premium pricing is determined through Google Play.

The price displayed to a user may vary depending on:

  • Country or region
  • Local currency
  • Applicable taxes
  • Exchange rates
  • Google Play's local pricing
  • Other applicable pricing requirements

The price displayed on Google Play at the time of purchase is the applicable purchase price.

CatalystCore Studios may change Premium pricing in the future, subject to applicable platform requirements.

11. Free Usage and Advertising

The App may provide certain functionality to free users subject to usage limits.

Free users may also see advertisements.

The availability of specific free limits, Premium features, and advertising placements may change as the App evolves.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the services used by the App.

Because your documents and images are processed locally, the contents of those files are not transmitted to our own servers for processing.

However, no software, device, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should maintain appropriate backups of important documents.

13. Data Retention

We do not retain copies of your PDFs, images, scanned documents, or other User Content on our servers because the App does not upload that content to our servers for processing.

Information processed by third-party services such as Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising services, and Google Play may be retained according to their respective policies and configurations.

14. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App or through a service associated with the App, please contact us.

15. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services including:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, if enabled
  • Google ML Kit
  • Google Play Billing

These services are operated by third parties and may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

16. Your Choices

Depending on your device, operating system, account settings, and applicable laws, you may have controls over certain advertising, analytics, and privacy settings.

You can also choose which files to provide to the App and which files to save through Android's file-selection mechanisms.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its features, monetization model, or data practices change.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage users to periodically review this Privacy Policy.
